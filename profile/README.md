## About us

**Luna-Rigel-Systems** is a software development initiative focused on building practical, maintainable digital tools for commercial operations.

Our work connects desktop software, mobile applications, and web experiences within the **SYMMETRA** ecosystem. We favor thoughtful engineering, clear workflows, and continuous improvement over unnecessary complexity.

## What we're working on

| Initiative | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **SYMMETRA POS** | Point-of-sale workflows, inventory management, and operational tooling. |
| **SYMMETRA Scanner** | Android-assisted scanning and integration with retail workflows. |
| **SYMMETRA Web** | E-commerce experiences and digital storefront development. |

These initiatives are at different stages of development. Their inclusion here does not indicate general availability.

## Our approach

- **Practical engineering:** solve real operational problems.
- **Reliability:** prioritize consistency, maintainability, and clear system behavior.
- **Connected workflows:** design desktop, mobile, and web components to work together where appropriate.
- **Iterative development:** test, refine, document, and improve.
- **Responsible delivery:** safeguard operational information, credentials, and proprietary work.

## Repository access

Most development repositories are **private**. This public organization profile introduces our work; it is not a public source-code release or an invitation to redistribute proprietary materials.

Public repositories, documentation, and contribution opportunities may be provided individually when ready.
